## 🌐 Navigation

1. **Home Page** - Public landing page (no login required)
   - Click "Ingia Mfumo" to go to login

2. **Login** - Select role, enter username and password

3. **Dashboard** - Automatically routed based on your role

---

## 📊 What Each Role Can Do

### 🔧 Admin
- View system-wide metrics and analytics
- Monitor all suppliers, retailers, and cooperatives
- Access regional overview
- Track all activities

### 📦 Supplier
- Dispatch fertilizer batches
- Track inventory
- View delivery status
- Access dispatch history

### 🏪 Retailer
- Receive fertilizer from suppliers
- Distribute to farmers (with OTP)
- View farmer registry
- Manage stock

### 🤝 Cooperative (AMCOS)
- Manage member farmers
- Receive fertilizer
- Distribute to farmers (with OTP)
- Collect coffee from farmers
- Track all transactions

---

## 👨‍🌾 Farmer Data (No Login)

Farmers are identified by Ministry IDs (e.g., MOA-KAG-001). They don't have accounts but their data is in the system for tracking.

Sample Farmer IDs:
- MOA-KAG-001 to MOA-KAG-005 (Bukoba - AMCOS-001)
- MOA-KAG-006 to MOA-KAG-010 (Karagwe - AMCOS-002)
- MOA-KAG-011 to MOA-KAG-015 (Muleba - AMCOS-003)
